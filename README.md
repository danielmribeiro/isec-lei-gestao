# Gestão (Management)
Repository for the "Gestão" course at Instituto Superior de Engenharia de Coimbra (ISEC), part of the "Licenciatura em Engenharia Informática" degree program. This repository contains exercises, resources, and materials related to the study of Management

## Learning Objectives:
- Develop knowledge and understanding in the field of Management, covering the various functional areas within a company.
- Apply theoretical concepts to the real world, fostering the ability to analyze reality from a managerial perspective.
- Acquire knowledge that enables Computer Engineers to communicate effectively with managers, as well as develop a critical, constructive, and creative vision for future development projects in their field.
- Understand the reality of management positions.
- Develop the ability to apply acquired knowledge to solving specific business problems, supported by independent reasoning.
- Enhance creativity and entrepreneurial skills.

## Competencies:
- Identify and evaluate the relationships between the Organization and its environment and how they influence each other.
- Identify and interpret the various functions of an Organization.
- Understand and apply the main strategic analysis methods of an Organization.
- Understand Marketing and its strategic importance to an Organization.
- Understand the determinants of motivation for various stakeholders of an Organization.
- Construct a business plan applicable to an idea.
- Show a critical attitude toward practical case studies of an Organization.
- Communicate opinions and ideas clearly and simply.
- Develop autonomous and group work capacities.

## Course Content:

### Theoretical:
- Organization and company
- Organizational theories
- Companies and their environment
- Business strategy
- Structure of companies
- Human resources
- Leadership
- Marketing

### Practical:
- Business planning
- Strategic analysis
    - Environmental analysis
    - Company analysis
- Strategy formulation
- Organization and implementation of strategy
- Introduction to financial and management accounting

## Assessment Criteria
**General Assessment System:**
- Final exam (written test) at the end of the semester = 100%

OR

**Continuous Assessment System:**
- Practical assignment = 40%
- Final exam (written test) at the end of the semester = 60%

**Conditions for Continuous Assessment:**
- A minimum attendance of 75% in practical classes is required.
- Group assignments must be submitted via Moodle and presented in practical classes. Each group must consist of five students, and all members must participate in the presentation/defense of their work.
- A minimum grade of 7 out of 20 is required in the final exam.
